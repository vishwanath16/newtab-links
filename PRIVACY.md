# Privacy Policy: Links in New Tab

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

Links in New Tab ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that opens clicked links in a new browser tab.

## Data we collect
**None.** The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or browsing data.

## What the extension does locally
- When you click a link on a web page, the extension reads that link's URL in your browser and immediately uses it to open a new tab. The URL is not saved, logged or sent anywhere.
- It stores one setting, your on/off preference, using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync` API. This value is kept by Chrome and may sync across your own signed-in Chrome browsers. We have no access to it.

## Permissions
- **storage**: saves your on/off preference.
- **Access to all websites (content script on all URLs)**: needed to detect link clicks on any page you visit. It is used only to open the clicked link in a new tab.

## Third parties
The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, no ads and no third-party services, and loads no remote code.

## Children's privacy
The extension collects no data from anyone, including children.
